Overview

Trustworthiness beyond the model boundary

This workshop brings together researchers in trustworthy machine learning, AI safety, system security, and governance to address risks that emerge when foundation models act as autonomous agents.

System-level focus

Agentic AI systems interact with external tools, access local resources, and execute multi-step operations. Trustworthiness must account for models, infrastructure, human oversight, and deployment context together.

Interdisciplinary community

The workshop aims to connect machine learning, systems engineering, cybersecurity, human-agent interaction, and governance communities around practical frameworks for reliable agentic systems.

Asia-Pacific opportunity

With NeurIPS 2026 planned in Australia, the workshop will amplify perspectives from the Asia-Pacific region while convening global academic, industry, and public-sector stakeholders.

Topics

Research themes

We welcome contributions that examine how agentic AI systems can be evaluated, controlled, secured, and governed in real-world environments.

Agentic Safety & Control

Monitoring, intervention, and override mechanisms for autonomous agent behavior.

System-Level Robustness

How model failures propagate through pipelines, tools, and operational environments.

Interoperability & Security

Trustworthy integration with software systems, APIs, enterprise infrastructure, and IoT.

Human-Agent Collaboration

Transparency, accountability, trust calibration, and shared control in socio-technical systems.

Evaluation & Benchmarking

Stress tests and benchmarks for dynamic, tool-using agentic systems beyond static datasets.

Regulatory & Ethical Frameworks

Alignment with safety standards, legal requirements, governance practice, and public accountability.
